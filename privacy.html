TRANSPARENCY & PRIVACY

Privacy notice.

Updated October 13, 2026

We respect your privacy and believe you should know what happens when you share your details using our website.

Information collected

When you submit the contact form, we collect your email address, optional name or message, submission time and consent acknowledgment. When you place a checkout order, we additionally collect your name, email, optional mobile number and notes, the selected payment method, the offer name and amount, payment reference (if supplied), order reference and fulfillment/payment statuses. We do not request, process or store payment card numbers, card PINs, passwords or OTPs.

How your information is used

We use inquiry details to respond to your questions. Order details are used to track pending purchases, manually verify transfers if applicable and coordinate fulfillment. Submitting an order does not charge you automatically or opt you into an advertising mailing list.

Storage and access

Inquiries and orders are stored using persistent storage attached to this website's Railway hosting service. Authorized website administrators can review and manage records in a protected dashboard. Buyers can review only their own order using its private reference and access link. Hosting and network providers process data needed to operate the service.

Requesting updates or deletion

You may send an inquiry requesting access, correction or deletion of a previous submission, referencing the email address used. The website operator may need to verify your identity before acting.

External video and checkout services

Our website may include third-party embedded video players or links to an external payment or booking provider. When you choose to use these services, their terms and privacy policies also apply.

This notice describes the website's current technical functionality. The website operator should customize the business contact information and legally required disclosures for the location and business before running a commercial advertising campaign.
