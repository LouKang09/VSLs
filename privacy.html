INFORMATION & PRIVACY

Privacy notice.

Updated October 13, 2026.

When you contact this website using the inquiry form, we collect the information you choose to provide: your name (optional), email address, message (optional), consent confirmation, and the time you submitted it.

Why we collect it

We use these details to respond to your inquiry about the offer. A form submission does not create an order, enroll you in a recurring newsletter, or authorize payment.

Where information is stored

Inquiries are saved in persistent storage associated with this website's Railway hosting service. They are visible only in the password-protected administrator dashboard, not on the public sales page. Hosting and network providers may process the data needed to deliver this service.

How to get in touch

To ask about, update, or request deletion of information submitted through the form, submit a new inquiry on the main website, using the email address you originally provided, and clearly describe your request. The website operator will need to verify and handle the request.

Payment and external links

The website may direct you to a separate checkout, booking, or video platform chosen by the website operator. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices. This site does not process card payments in its own server.

Important note

Only submit information you are comfortable sharing. This notice describes the website's current inquiry functionality and should be reviewed by the business operator before collecting customer data at scale.
